# Code of Conduct

OpenCourts.fyi is a volunteer, public-interest project. We want a community that is welcoming, respectful, and safe for contributors and users.

## Expectations

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent; ask clarifying questions.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementations, not people.
- Protect privacy and safety; do not post sensitive personal information.
- Disclose when automated systems (including AI agents) are participating.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Discriminatory, hateful, or demeaning language.
- Threats of violence or encouragement of harm.
- Sharing private information (doxxing) or facilitating it.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it by opening a GitHub issue labeled "conduct" (if safe to do so) or by contacting a maintainer privately.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other actions needed to protect the community.
